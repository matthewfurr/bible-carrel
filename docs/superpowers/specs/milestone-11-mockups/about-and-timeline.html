A book's About page: the head and the timeline

Genesis, with its real data. Two choices on this page: how the top of the page reads (1 or 2), and which timeline (A, B or C). Click one of each, or tell me in the terminal.

The same in every option: events in the order they happened (your answer). The "Birth of / Lifetime of / Death of" entries fold into one lifetime each, so Genesis's 116 entries come down to 25 lifetimes and 43 events. Eras the data already names (The Great Flood, Sojourn in Canaan and Egypt, Holy Week, the missionary journeys) group their events. "Death of Moses" loses Genesis 34:1, an error in Theographic's own data. With Show dates off: the same order, with no years and no scale.
What all of this needs from the data: each event's length and era. Theographic has both for every event; today's import leaves them out. That means a rebuilt Bible database, not your notes, so nothing you have stored changes.
