1. The missing pin (a fix, one proposal)

The likely cause: places at the same spot share one pin, named after the most-mentioned of them. In Acts, Judea, Solomon's Portico, the Beautiful Gate and Akeldama all sit on Jerusalem's spot, so choosing Solomon's Portico shows a pin that says "Jerusalem". If you remember a place that showed no pin, tell me which: a live look will confirm the cause before anything is built.

What changes: a shared pin takes the name of whichever of its places is chosen. The chosen pin is always drawn above its neighbours (Jerusalem, the Kidron and the Gihon are a few hundred metres apart and can hide one another). On the phone the map moves the chosen pin clear of the list's sheet, which it does not do today. Cost: small.

2. Ordering the list

The list gets the same switch as People, with a third choice: Most named (today's order), A to Z, and By chapter. By chapter puts each chapter's places under its number, in the order the chapter first names them. A place named in several chapters is listed in each: in Acts, 43 of the 101 places are. Which way should the map behave under By chapter?
