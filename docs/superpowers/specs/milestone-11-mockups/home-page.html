What changes: book names in the page face at the size the page is set to (14 to 28 pt), with the Testament titles and group labels in proportion. The columns widen to hold the longest name ("2 Thessalonians") at that size, so a larger size means fewer columns. Below four, the Testaments stack, as they already do in a narrow window. The chosen book's wash, the marked dot and the keyboard all stay as they are. Cost: small; the column-break rule needs no change.

2. The pane, dragged wider: what fills the room?

A divider between the contents and the pane drags like a split view, from today's 260 pt up to about 40% of the window. The width is remembered on this Mac only, as a device preference, the way your page size is; nothing in your notes changes. Please approve that stored value with your choice.
