A note on a verse you tap (iPhone)

John 15:5 has a yellow highlight and a note saved from the assistant, with references in it. Today a tap shows the edit bar, and Edit Note opens the raw markdown. Three ways to show the note as a note, with Edit one tap away. Click the closest.

A · The note opens first

Tapping a highlight that has a note opens its note at half height, rendered, references as links. Edit is top right; the mark's actions (color, bookmark, Ask, delete) sit along the foot. A tapped reference opens inside the sheet, with Back. A highlight without a note keeps today's edit bar.

JOHN · CHAPTER 15
4Remain in me, and I in you. As the branch can’t bear fruit by itself unless it remains in the vine, so neither can you, unless you remain in me. 5I am the vine. You are the branches. He who remains in me and I in him bears much fruit,▤ for apart from me you can do nothing.
✕John 15:5Edit
“I am the vine. You are the branches.”

Q: What does “remain” mean here?

“Remain” (menō) is ongoing dependence, the same word as John 15:4 and 1 John 2:6. The fruit is the character of Galatians 5:22–23, not activity.
