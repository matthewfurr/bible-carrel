Mac: where should the list of shortcuts appear?

⌘/ (or Help › Keyboard Shortcuts) opens it. Both hold every shortcut in the app, grouped the same way. Click the one you want.

A. A sheet over the reader my pick

Everything at a glance in three columns, with a field to filter. It comes down over the window you are in and goes away with Esc, Done or ⌘/ again. While it is up you cannot use the reader.

B. A small window of its own

A narrow list you can leave open beside the reader while you learn the keys, and close when you know them. The reader stays usable. It is one more window to place, and with AeroSpace it would be tiled like any other.
