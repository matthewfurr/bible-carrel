  • Old Testament and New Testament head the two halves, with a book count each; the nine groups sit under them.
  • A translation with the Apocrypha (WEB, CPDV) gets a third heading, Apocrypha, after the New Testament.
  • Click a book: its name washes green and the pane at the right shows its icons and chapters. Click a chapter to read. A double-click on a book opens its first chapter.
  • A small dot after a book’s name means it has your marks in it, as the chapter dots do today.
  • The search field moves to the toolbar, since the sidebar is gone. Typing still filters the books and offers “Go to Romans 8”.
  • Arrow keys move through the books and then the chapters; Return opens.
  • At large text sizes, or in a small window, the contents page scrolls; the pane stays put.
