iPhone: what should a tap on a verse do?

In each picture John 15:5 has just been tapped once. Verse 4 already has a yellow highlight. Press and hold still picks out single words in all three, as it does today. Click the one you want.

A. Select it like text

The verse is selected with a handle at each end, and today’s menu floats over it.

  • Drag a handle in for part of the verse, or out across more verses.
  • Smallest change: it is the menu you already tuned.
  • The menu covers a line of text on every tap, and has no room left: Compare and References would sit behind the arrow.

B. Mark the verse, actions in a bar

The verse takes the pale green wash and a bar replaces the tab bar, as the pen’s bar does.

  • Nothing covers the text. Every action is named, with room for Compare and References.
  • Tap more verses to add them (John 15:5–6).
  • No handles: part of a verse is only by press and hold, then today’s menu.

C. Handles and the bar my pick

The verse is selected with handles, and the actions sit in the bar instead of a floating menu.

  • One tap takes the verse; a drag on a handle takes part of it or several verses.
  • Nothing covers the text, and Compare and References have a place.
  • Biggest change: the floating menu you tuned is replaced by the bar for every selection, press and hold included.
