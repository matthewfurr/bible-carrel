A. Grouped sidebar, one book on the page my pick

The sidebar you have, with the books folded into their groups; the group holding your book is open. Click a book and the page shows that book alone: its name, its icons, its chapters. Nothing long to scroll, and the page has room for People, Places and About to open right there later in this project.

B. One column that opens in place

No sidebar. Groups open to their books, and a book opens to its icons and chapters under its name, as the iPhone’s Library does; opening another closes the first. Closest to what you described. The page is one narrow column in a wide window, and People, Places and About would need to open somewhere else.

C. A contents page

Every book on one page, set out by group like a printed Bible’s contents, with the chosen book’s chapters in a pane beside it. Nothing scrolls and nothing is folded away. It is the biggest change from today, and at large text sizes the page would have to scroll after all.
