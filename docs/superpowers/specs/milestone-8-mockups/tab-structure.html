What sits in the tab bar on iPhone?

All three use the system Liquid Glass tab bar. It floats over the page, shrinks out of the way as you scroll the chapter, and the search button turns into a search field when tapped. The difference is what gets a tab and where asking a question lives. Click the one that fits; tell me in the chat what you'd change.

John 15

1I am the true vine, and my Father is the husbandman. 2Every branch in me that beareth not fruit he taketh away: and every branch that beareth fruit, he purgeth it, that it may bring forth more fruit. 3Now ye are clean through the word which I have spoken unto you. 4Abide in me, and I in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit of itself, except it abide in the vine; no more can ye, except ye abide in me. 5I am the vine, ye are the branches: He that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me ye can do nothing. 6If a man abide not in me, he is cast forth as a branch, and is withered; and men gather them, and cast them into the fire, and they are burned. 7If ye abide in me, and my words abide in you, ye shall ask what ye will, and it shall be done unto you.

A · Read, Notes, Ask + Search

One tab per job. Books and chapters open from the button beside the title.

Read the chapter, where you left it. Notes everything you've marked, with search and the time filter. Ask a question with the keyboard ready, past conversations below. Search jumps to a reference or finds words in the Bible and in your notes.

B · Read, Notes + Search, Ask above the bar

Asking is never a tab away: a glass strip rides above the bar on every screen and already knows the passage you're on.

Tap it and the conversation comes up as a sheet over the page. When the bar shrinks on scroll, the strip tucks in beside it. Past conversations live inside the sheet. Costs a strip of the page while the bar is showing.

C · Library, Read, Notes, Ask + Search

The fullest bar. The bookshelf from the Mac's home page gets its own tab instead of hiding behind the title.

Library the shelf of books, chapter grids, and where you left off. The other four are as in A. Five targets is the most an iPhone bar holds, so the labels run tight.

Sample text is the King James Version. Marks in the right margin show a note (verse 4) and a bookmark (verse 5), the way the Mac's gutter does.
