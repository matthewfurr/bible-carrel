John 15
What does "abide" mean in the Greek?

The word is menō: to remain, stay, or dwell. John uses it about forty times.

Early on it is plain staying: the Spirit remains on Jesus (John 1:32). By chapter 15 it means a settled life in him, and John later makes it the test of a disciple (1 John 2:6).
