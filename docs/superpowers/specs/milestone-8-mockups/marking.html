A · Everything in the bar

A second glass row rises above the colors with Note, Bookmark, Ask and Delete. Tapping another color recolors the mark.

All of it under your thumb and nothing ever covers the verse. Takes the most of the page while a mark is selected.

B · Colors in the bar, actions under the mark

Both of your ideas together. Colors stay at the bottom; Note, Bookmark, Ask and Delete pop up right under the words.

The actions are where your eyes already are. The popup covers a line or two below the mark until you pick or tap away.

C · Everything under the mark

The tab bar never changes, even with the pen on. Colors and actions come up together beside the words.

Closest to how other iPhone apps do it, and you can still jump to Notes or Ask mid-marking. The biggest popup over the text, and no color is picked ahead of the drag: the pen uses the last one.

With the pen off, pressing and holding on a word selects text the usual iPhone way and offers the same choices, plus Copy. Sample text is the King James Version.
