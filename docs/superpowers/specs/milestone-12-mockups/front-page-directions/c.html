Bible Carrel

Build it from sourceRead the guide ›

Not in the App Store and not downloadable. You build it yourself from its source, with Xcode.

The page

1One chapter at a time, set like a printed page as far as each translation's text allows: paragraphs that indent and poetry that hangs under its first word. 2Four page styles, Printed, Lectern, Study and Classic, each in light, sepia and dark.

Marks and notes

3Highlight a phrase, not only a verse, in colors you choose. 4Write a note beside it, or bookmark the verse. 5Every delete can be undone.

The Library

6Each of the 66 books opens to the people it names, with a family tree on the Mac. 7A map shows its places, and a timeline sets its events in the order of history.

Search

8⌘K on the Mac, and the search button on the iPhone, search the Bible, your notes, highlights, bookmarks and conversations at once. 9On the Mac the same field goes to a passage and runs commands.
