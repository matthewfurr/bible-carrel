Bible Carrel

Build it from sourceRead the guide ›

Not in the App Store and not downloadable. You build it yourself from its source, with Xcode.

It reads like a printed Bible

1One chapter at a time, set like a printed page as far as each translation's text allows: paragraphs that indent and poetry that hangs under its first word. 2Four page styles, Printed, Lectern, Study and Classic, each in light, sepia and dark.

How the reader works ›

Pencil in the margin

2Highlight a phrase, not only a verse, in colors you choose. 2Write a note beside it, or bookmark the verse. 3Every delete can be undone.

Highlights and bookmarks ›

People, places and each book's story

3Each of the 66 books opens to the people it names, with a family tree on the Mac. 2A map shows its places, and a timeline sets its events in the order of history.

The Library ›

One field finds anything

4⌘K on the Mac, and the search button on the iPhone, search the Bible, your notes, highlights, bookmarks and conversations at once. 2On the Mac the same field goes to a passage and runs commands.

Search and ⌘K ›

The same desk in your pocket

5The iPhone app is the same reader, laid out for the phone. 2With iCloud sync set up, it shares your notes with the Mac.
