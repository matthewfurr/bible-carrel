Bible Carrel

A study desk for reading through the Bible
❦

1Bible Carrel is a reader for the Mac and the iPhone that sets a chapter as a printed Bible would: a measure, margins, paragraphs that indent and poetry that hangs. It opens where you left off and shows one chapter at a time, with nothing on the page that the text did not earn.

Marks and notes

2Highlight a phrase, not just a verse, in colours you name, and write a note beside it. Bookmark a verse. Every mark is kept in your own iCloud and reaches your other devices; every delete can be undone.

The Notes window lists everything you have marked across the whole Bible, by day or in Bible order, and exports it as Markdown.

The Library

3Each book opens to its people, with a drawn family tree; its places, on a map; and a timeline in the order of history.

Colophon

Set in Iowan Old Style and Literata. The Bible text is eight English translations, each named with its holder; the cross-references are OpenBible.info’s; the people, places and events are Theographic Bible Metadata’s.

Reading

The reader

One chapter at a time, set like a printed page.

The reader shows one chapter. Chapter 1 opens on the book’s title; each later chapter opens on its own label. As the opening scrolls away, a running head names the chapter, and the thin line under it grows as you read.

Turning chapters

Scrolling stops at the chapter’s end, where two links go back or on. From the keyboard:

⌥⌘→Next chapter
⌥⌘←Previous chapter
⌘KGo to any passage
⇧⌘HHome

Your place

The app remembers the chapter and verse you are reading and reopens there on every device. Looking something up does not move it: Back returns you to where you were.
