# Milestone 11 ledgers

The ledgers of milestone 11's five projects (UI improvements), each the git-ignored `.superpowers/sdd/<plan>/progress.md` of its workspace, copied here at the end of its project by its documents step: projects 1 to 4 on 2026-11-23, project 5 on 2026-11-24. They were copied because the workspace is git-ignored and goes with its worktree when the worktree is removed, and a ledger holds what no commit does: every ruling made on Matt's behalf in the unattended run, with what it costs if wrong, the small findings put off and where they went, and what was seen live and by whom.

Nothing else from the workspaces was kept. The task briefs are copies of the plan, the diff packages are copies of git, and what mattered in the task reports and reviews went into the ledger and the spec's "As built" section. A ledger still names those files (`task-5-report.md`, `task-9-rereview-1.md`, `preflight.md` and the like); the commits it names are in git.

Each ledger is named for its plan in `docs/superpowers/plans/`; its spec is `docs/superpowers/specs/<plan's name>-design.md`. The ranges run from the plan's commit to the commit that recorded the project finished, on `milestone-11-ui-improvements` unless named.
