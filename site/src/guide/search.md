---
title: Search and ⌘K
lede: One field for a passage, a word, a note or a command.
---

<kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>K</kbd> on the Mac (Go › Go to Reference…, or the magnifying glass in the toolbar), and Search on the iPhone, search the Bible, your notes, highlights, bookmarks and conversations at once, in the translation you are reading. In <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>K</kbd> you can also go to a passage or run a command. The commands include the menus' own, plus the page styles, the appearances, Switch Translation… and Copy Link to This Chapter. Each shows its shortcut.

{% shot "search", "The search field open over John 1 with two words typed and a list of Scripture results with the matched words underlined", "lazy", "(min-width: 65rem) 43rem, 100vw" %}

<kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>K</kbd> is empty each time it opens, however it was closed. Under Recent it shows what you read, looked up and opened lately, and under that six commands. Each device keeps its newest twenty, and they never go to iCloud. Clear Recents empties the list.

## Five forms

<div class="table-scroll">

| Type | Finds |
|---|---|
| `remain fruit` | Verses with both words, in any order, anywhere in the verse. |
| `abid` | Words that start with it: abide, abides, abideth, abiding. Every bare word works this way, so the results narrow as you type. Case and accents do not matter. |
| `"living water"` | Those words, next to each other, in that order (curly quotes work). |
| `love -fear` | Verses with love and without the word fear; `-"a phrase"` leaves out a phrase. |
| `beer-sheba` | A hyphen inside a word joins it; it is not a minus. |

</div>

Words are matched by their letters, not by meaning, so `love` finds love, loved, loves and loveth but not "loving". `lov` finds all of them. A word with an apostrophe, such as `peter's`, finds the possessive. `OR`, `NOT`, `NEAR`, `*` and `:` are plain text here, not operators.

Your notes, highlights and conversations follow the same rules. The Notes window's own field does not: it also matches inside words and by reference. See [The Notes window]({{ '/guide/notes-window/' | url }}).

Matched words are bold and underlined in every verse, quote and note line, on both apps.

## Narrowing the results

A menu beside the results limits Scripture to the Old Testament, the New Testament, the Apocrypha (in a translation that has it), the book you are reading or any one book. It returns to Whole Bible each time <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>K</kbd> opens, and when the field is emptied. On the Mac, <kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>Tab</kbd> step it through Whole Bible, the testaments, the Apocrypha and the book you are reading. <kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>Tab</kbd> narrow the results to one kind.

The count beside Scripture is the number of verses in that scope. The best match comes first.

## When nothing is found

When Scripture finds nothing, a near spelling is offered, such as "Did you mean “abide”?" with its verse count, and <kbd>Return</kbd> runs it. If nothing else matched either, it is the only row. If a book, a command, a note or anything else did match, the near spelling comes last, so a command typed by name still runs on <kbd>Return</kbd>.

Nothing is offered for a query with quotes, an excluded word, a word with an apostrophe or hyphen, a reference, a word with a digit, or more than twelve words.

## Names and passages

Type a reference or a book to go there. A person or a place is found by its name, or by a name it is also called, and opens its page for the whole Bible. See [The Library]({{ '/guide/library/' | url }}). Notes that match what you type are offered too.

## On the iPhone

Search is one field for everything: a reference or book, the words of the Bible, your notes and your conversations, grouped. Chips narrow it to one kind, including the Library's People and Places. A name typed opens that person's or place's page for the whole Bible, inside Search.

{% shot "phone-search", "Search on the iPhone with two words typed and a list of Scripture results with the matched words underlined", "lazy", "(min-width: 900px) 16rem, 50vw", "shot-phone" %}

It takes the five forms above. Menus set where to search (Whole Bible, a testament, a book) and, on the Scripture chip, the order: Best match or Bible order. With nothing typed it lists what you read and looked up lately, under Recent, with Clear. It has no commands. With a hardware keyboard, <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>K</kbd> opens it from any tab.
