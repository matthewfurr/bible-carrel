title: The iPhone app
lede: The same reader and the same notes, laid out for the phone.
---

The iPhone app is the same reader as the Mac's. With sync on, it shares your notes with the Mac. It is laid out for the iPhone only. The tabs are Read, Plan, Notes and Ask, with the Search button beside them.

## Read

Read shows the chapter. The bar at the top has:

- the Library button, which lists the books (see [The Library]({{ '/guide/library/' | url }}))
- the running head, the chapter and the translation, as the bar's title
- the highlighter
- the ••• menu, with rows of buttons for page style, text size, line spacing and appearance (Light, Sepia or Dark), then the translation, Notes in This Chapter and Settings

Settings ends with About, the same list of sources as the Mac's.

A verse with a note or a bookmark carries a mark in the right margin. Tap a note's mark to open it. [Notes]({{ '/guide/notes/' | url }}) describes the mark for a chapter's own notes. Every chapter ends with Previous and Next links, crossing into the neighboring book at either end.

For the book thumb, which is the right edge of the screen, and the chapter switcher, which is past each end of the page, see [The reader]({{ '/guide/reader/' | url }}).

## The markup menu

Tap a verse to select it, with the markup menu over it. Press and hold to select words instead. The menu highlights, adds notes, bookmarks, copies, shares or asks about the selection.

It leads with one circle in a color: the highlight's, when the words are inside one, and otherwise the pen's. Tap it for the list of your colors. The actions after it are symbols. The list behind the menu's chevron names each one, with Look Up and Translate last.

## The pen

The pen is quicker for a run of marks. Tap the highlighter and the tab bar becomes a row of your colors. Hold a word for a moment and drag to paint. A drag that starts moving straight away scrolls instead.

{% shot "phone-pen", "The iPhone reader on John 1 with the pen on: the tab bar is replaced by a row of five highlight colors and a check mark", "lazy", "(min-width: 900px) 16rem, 50vw", "shot-phone" %}

Tap a mark to select it. Handles at its ends resize it. The menu under it leads with the mark's color: a color chosen there recolors the mark and becomes the pen's. The menu also adds a note, bookmarks the verse, asks about it or deletes it. Done (the check mark) turns the pen off.

The pen is hidden while VoiceOver is running, since the markup menu does everything it does.

## Plan

The Plan tab sits between Read and Notes, with this week at the head. See [Reading plans]({{ '/guide/reading-plans/' | url }}).

## Notes

The Notes tab lists everything you have marked, with menus to narrow it. A swipe deletes, and a shake or a three-finger swipe undoes. See [The Notes window]({{ '/guide/notes-window/' | url }}).

## Ask

Ask is the study assistant, with the Mac's prompts and conversations. Ask About This Passage on a selection opens it over the page. See [The study assistant]({{ '/guide/assistant/' | url }}).

## Search

Search is one field for a reference or book, the words of the Bible, your notes and your conversations. See [Search and ⌘K]({{ '/guide/search/' | url }}).

With a hardware keyboard, <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>K</kbd> opens Search from any tab, and <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>[</kbd> and <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>]</kbd> go back and forward on the Read tab.

## Your place

Looking something up keeps your place, as on the Mac. A row in Notes, a mark or verse in Search, or a reference in an answer opens the passage with your chapter's name at the top left as the way back. A passage opened from the Library names the person, place or view you came from. Reading on with Next or Previous, or Read From Here in the ••• menu, makes the passage your place. So does scrolling five lines or more from a jump inside the chapter you are reading. See [Your place]({{ '/guide/your-place/' | url }}).

## What stays with each device

Your notes, your place and your synced settings travel between the Mac and the iPhone when sync is on. Text size, line spacing, column width, appearance, the Notes tab's time filter and the pen's color stay with each device. See [Sync and backups]({{ '/guide/sync-and-backups/' | url }}).

Settings › General has Your Data, with Export All Data… and Import Data… in it. See [Export and import]({{ '/guide/export-and-import/' | url }}).
