## Add a note

Select a verse or some words and choose Add Note in the markup menu, or press <kbd>N</kbd> with a verse selected. <kbd>N</kbd> also shows the notes column. A note added without a color anchors to the phrase with a dotted underline. You can add a color later from the same menu.

## The notes column

The notes column is the window's inspector, on the right. Drag its edge to resize it, and press <kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>N</kbd> to hide or show it. It lists this chapter's notes and, as one-line rows, its highlights without notes.

{% shot "notes-column", "John 1 with the second note selected in the notes column and its highlight outlined on the page", "lazy", "(min-width: 65rem) 43rem, 100vw" %}

A first click on a row selects it, scrolls to its highlight and outlines it. A second click, or <kbd>Return</kbd>, edits the note, or starts one on a highlight. <kbd>Esc</kbd> or <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>Return</kbd> finishes. To finish with <kbd>Return</kbd> alone, change "Finish a note with" in Settings › General.

With the column focused, <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> move between rows and <kbd>Delete</kbd> deletes. <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>Z</kbd> brings back what you deleted; see [Highlights and bookmarks]({{ '/guide/highlights/' | url }}).

## Chapter notes

A note can belong to a whole chapter rather than to a verse. The study assistant's Add as Note makes one from a chapter, book or general conversation. A chapter with notes of its own shows a paper mark at the right of its opening line, with the count inside. The running head carries the mark too while that chapter is in view. Click the mark to show the notes column, where those notes come first.

## Links in a note

A passage link written into a note, such as `bible://jhn.3.16`, is a link in the rendered note. Copy Link in the markup menu copies the link to the selected verses. See [Passage links]({{ '/guide/passage-links/' | url }}).

## On the iPhone

A verse with a note carries a mark in the right margin. Tap the mark to open the note. A chapter's own notes show as a counted paper mark on its opening line, which opens Notes in This Chapter. Everything you have marked is in the Notes tab, described in [The Notes window]({{ '/guide/notes-window/' | url }}).

## Where notes are kept

With sync on, notes reach your other devices. See [Sync and backups]({{ '/guide/sync-and-backups/' | url }}).
