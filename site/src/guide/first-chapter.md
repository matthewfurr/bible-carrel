On the Mac, Bible Carrel opens on Home. Every book of the translation is listed under its Testament and its group (Law, History, Poetry & Wisdom and so on), in as many columns as fit. Beside the list is a pane for the chapters of the book you have chosen.

{% shot "home", "Home on the Mac: this week's reading plan above the books under each Testament, with John chosen and its chapters in the pane at the right", "lazy", "(min-width: 65rem) 43rem, 100vw" %}

## Choose a book and a chapter

When the app opens, the book you were reading is chosen and its chapters are in the pane at the right. Click another book to see its chapters. Double-click a book to open it. Click a chapter to read it.

To find a book by typing, use the field in the toolbar (<kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>F</kbd>). Type a reference such as `rom 8`: Romans is chosen, with chapter 8 outlined in the accent color. Press <kbd>Return</kbd> to open it.

A ring around a chapter number means the chapter has a highlight, a note or a bookmark. [The Library]({{ '/guide/library/' | url }}) explains its colors.

The names and numbers on Home follow your text size. <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>+</kbd> and <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>−</kbd> make them larger and smaller. Drag the divider between the list and the chapters to give the chapters more room, and double-click the divider to return the pane to its narrowest. The width is kept on this Mac only.

## Choose a translation

The translation you are reading is named in the toolbar, such as WEB. Click it to switch to another. Settings › General › Default translation sets the one the app starts with.

## The chapter

The reader shows one chapter at a time. The first chapter of a book opens on the book's title. Every other chapter opens straight on its text.

The running head at the top of the page names the chapter. A thin line under it grows as you read and is full at the chapter's foot. Click the running head to go back to the book's chapters.

At the end of the chapter are two links, Previous and Next, each naming the chapter it goes to, such as "Next: John 4". From the keyboard, <kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>→</kbd> opens the next chapter and <kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>←</kbd> the previous one.

To come back to Home from anywhere, press <kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>H</kbd> or click the house in the toolbar. The book you are reading is chosen again.

## On the iPhone

The app opens on the Read tab, at the chapter you were reading. The Library button at the left of the bar lists the same books. See [The iPhone app]({{ '/guide/iphone/' | url }}).

## Close the app and open it again

The app remembers your place, the chapter and verse you were reading, and reopens there. [Your place]({{ '/guide/your-place/' | url }}) says what moves it and what does not.
