## Open it

On the Mac, open the Notes window with the Notes Browser button in the toolbar, with View › Notes Browser, with <kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>N</kbd>, or with "Notes Browser" in <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>K</kbd>.

## What you see

The window lists every note, highlight, bookmark and assistant conversation across the Bible. The Sort menu sets the order:

- **Date:** newest first, under a heading for each day (Today, Yesterday, then the weekday and date). Each row shows its time.
- **Reference:** Bible order, under a heading for each book. Each row shows its date.

Filter the list to All, Notes, Highlights, Bookmarks or Threads. Kept versions joins the filters while there are any. The How far back menu, beside the filters, limits the list to the past week, month, three months or year.

Search with <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>F</kbd> by note text, quoted text or reference, such as "Matt 5" or "John 3:16", or by a conversation's title, questions and replies. <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>K</kbd> also offers the newest notes that match what you type.

Press <kbd>Return</kbd> or double-click to go to the item. The reader opens it in its translation, with its row lit in the notes column and the highlight outlined. You edit in the reader. <kbd>Delete</kbd>, or the context menu, removes an item after asking, and <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>Z</kbd> brings it back.

## Export notes

The window's Export button saves what the list shows as Markdown, so filter or search first to export only those. File › Export Notes… saves everything. [Export and import]({{ '/guide/export-and-import/' | url }}) describes the file.

## Kept versions

If two devices change the same note before they have synced, the app keeps both texts. The note shows the text that won, and under it a quiet mark that reads "2 versions". Click it to see both texts, each with its device, "Mac" or "iPhone", and its time. Nothing alerts you and nothing is red, so leave it as long as you like.

Three choices settle it:

- **Use This One** gives the note these words and keeps the other text.
- **Keep Both in the Note** puts the kept text under the note's.
- **Discard** removes the version. Edit › Undo brings it back.

If you delete a note on one device and edit it on another, the edited text stays as a kept version with no note. It is listed only under Kept versions. Restore as a Note puts it back on its passage, and Discard removes it.

## On the iPhone

The Notes tab lists everything you have marked. Three menus narrow it: how far back, what kind (Everything, Notes, Highlights, Bookmarks, Conversations, and Kept versions while there are any) and which order (Newest first or Bible order). A swipe deletes. A shake or a three-finger swipe undoes.

The ••• menu exports what is listed as Markdown, through the share sheet.

A note with more than one version shows the mark under its text, in the note sheet and in the Notes tab. Tap it to open the versions. A swipe on a kept version's row discards it, and a shake undoes that.
