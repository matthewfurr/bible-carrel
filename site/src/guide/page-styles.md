The page comes in four styles: Printed (the default, set in Literata), Lectern, Study and Classic. You choose one in Settings › Reading.

## What you can set

Beside the style, Settings › Reading holds:

- the font
- the size, from 14 to 28 pt
- line spacing: Compact, Normal or Relaxed
- the column width
- the layout: paragraphs, or a verse to a line
- verse numbers, shown or hidden
- section headings, shown or hidden
- justification
- the appearance: System, Light, Sepia or Dark

Sepia is one of the four appearances. Here it is under the Lectern style, which opens a chapter on a large numeral:

Each change applies to the open chapter at once. The column's width, the verse numbers and the space between paragraphs follow the font size.

## Text & Layout

<kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>T</kbd>, View › Text & Layout, or the Text & Layout button in the toolbar (AA) opens the same settings in a popover over the page. <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>+</kbd> and <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>−</kbd> make the text larger and smaller.

## Section headings

Section headings are the Berean Standard Bible's, shown with every translation except the Catholic Public Domain Version (CPDV), whose numbering differs.

## What syncs

Page style, font, layout, verse numbers, section headings and justification sync through iCloud, so your other devices use the same settings. Size, line spacing, column width and appearance stay with each device.

## On the iPhone

The reader's ••• menu holds page style, text size, line spacing and appearance (Light, Sepia or Dark). Settings, at the foot of the same menu, has the rest, and the System appearance.
