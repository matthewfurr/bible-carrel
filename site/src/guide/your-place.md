---
title: Your place
lede: The chapter and verse you are reading, kept for you.
---

The app remembers your place, the chapter and verse you are reading, and reopens there. With sync on, your other devices follow it too.

## What moves your place

Reading moves it: scrolling within a chapter, and going from one chapter to the next. So does choosing a chapter on Home, using Previous and Next, and choosing a reference or a book in <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>K</kbd>.

## What does not

Looking something up does not. These show you a passage and leave your place where it was:

- a note, highlight or bookmark opened from the Notes window or from <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>K</kbd>
- a verse found by searching in <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>K</kbd>
- the Open button on a verse in an assistant reply
- a [passage link]({{ '/guide/passage-links/' | url }})

To return, use Back (<kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>[</kbd>) or Home (<kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>H</kbd>). Back puts you at the verse you left.

A passage in the chapter you are already reading is a lookup too: the page scrolls to it, your place stays, and Back returns. If you then scroll five lines or more from where it landed, up or down, that spot becomes your place. A passage in another translation stays a lookup however far you scroll.

## Making a lookup your place

If you read on from a looked-up passage with Previous or Next, or choose Go › Read From Here, it becomes your place.

If you quit while looking something up, the app reopens at your place, not at the passage.

## Coming back to the Library

Back and Home also return from a passage opened in the Library, to the same book, view and person or place. On the iPhone the way back is at the top left, and it names the person, place or view you came from. Reading on with Next or Previous, or Read From Here, makes the passage your place instead.

The Library keeps its book, view and person or place until you quit, close the Library on the iPhone, or press Home on the Mac when you are not returning from one of its passages. The page's scroll position and the map's zoom are not kept.

## Another device

A place from another device moves your reader only if it arrives while the app is in the background, or before you have moved in this launch, and never while you are looking something up. Back returns from it.
