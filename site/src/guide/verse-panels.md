---
title: Verse panels
lede: Compare a verse across translations, or follow where it points.
---

Select a verse, or a run of verses in one chapter, to open either of two panels. On the Mac they are in the markup menu, and on the keys <kbd>C</kbd> and <kbd>R</kbd>. On the iPhone they are behind the chevron of the markup menu. The panels are separate, and neither moves your place: a row opens as a lookup, and Back returns you.

The panels name translations by their abbreviations: ESV, NASB, AMP, ERV, ASV, KJV, WEB and CPDV. The [Sources]({{ '/sources/' | url }}) page gives each one's full name.

## Compare Translations

Compare Translations (<kbd>C</kbd>) shows the verse in the translation you are reading, marked "reading", and then in each translation of your compare set, in the order the translation menu lists them. Part of a verse counts as the whole verse. For more than five verses it shows the first five and says so.

{% shot "compare", "The Compare Translations panel under John 1:1, showing the verse in the WEB, ASV and KJV one above the other", "lazy", "(min-width: 65rem) 43rem, 100vw" %}

A translation that lacks the verse says so, as in "Not in the ERV.", and cannot be opened. The CPDV numbers the Psalms differently from the others, so in Psalms its row says it may show a neighboring verse.

Click a row to see the verse in that translation. A click on the row marked "reading" only closes the panel.

The foot of the panel has three buttons:

- **Ask About the Differences** opens the assistant on the verses, with the Compare translations prompt and the rows shown. See [The study assistant]({{ '/guide/assistant/' | url }}).
- **Copy** copies every row as Copy with Reference writes it, one to a line.
- **Translations…** is the checklist of the compare set.

The same checklist is in Settings › General under Compare with. It begins with ESV, NASB, KJV and WEB, and it syncs between your devices.

## Cross-References

Cross-References (<kbd>R</kbd>) is about the selection's first verse. The chevrons beside its reference step through the chapter's verses.

{% shot "cross-references", "The Cross-References panel under John 1:1: six verses it points to, from Genesis 1:1 to 1 John 1:1-2, each with its opening words, then Show all (36), the count of verses that point here, Ask About These and the OpenBible.info credit", "lazy", "(min-width: 65rem) 43rem, 100vw" %}

It lists what the verse points to and which verses point to it. The links come from OpenBible.info, whose readers vote on each one, and the strongest come first. The panel shows six of the links with four or more votes, then Show all with the count. A link that runs into another chapter is shown as the part in its first chapter.

A row shows the reference over its opening words in the translation you are reading. Click it to see the passage inside the panel, with Back and Open. Ask About These opens the assistant on the verse with one question the app writes from the verse and the passages listed. The links are written into the Bible database when you build it, so the app never goes to the network for them.

## Closing them

On the Mac each panel is a popover on the selection's first line. <kbd>Esc</kbd> or a click outside closes it, and the selection stays. It also closes when the page scrolls under it or its text is laid out again, for example with a new text size, the notes column or a resized window. At large text sizes the foot of each panel wraps and the credit stays on screen.

## On the iPhone

Each panel is a sheet, at half height or full.
