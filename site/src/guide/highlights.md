## The markup menu

On the Mac, click a verse's words or number. The verse is selected and the markup menu opens just below it, or just above it when there is no room below, so it does not cover the words. Dragging across words, double-clicking a word, clicking inside a highlight, or right-clicking a selection opens it too.

{% shot "markup-menu", "The markup menu open under verse 5 of John 1, with a row of color swatches above Add Color…, Add Note, Bookmark Verse 5, Compare Translations, Cross-References and the copy commands", "lazy", "(min-width: 65rem) 43rem, 100vw" %}

The menu holds a row of color swatches, with `+` to add a color, and then Add Color…, Add Note, Bookmark Verse, Compare Translations, Cross-References, Copy, Copy with Reference, Copy Link, Share… and Ask About This Passage.

With the menu open, a click anywhere else only closes it and deselects. <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the menu and keeps the selection, so the keys below can act on it. A second <kbd>Esc</kbd> clears the selection.

Click a swatch to highlight the selection in that color. A note added without a color anchors to the phrase with a dotted underline, and you can add a color later from the same menu.

Copy with Reference puts the words and where they come from on the clipboard, in this form:

> “Blessed are the poor in spirit” — Matthew 5:3 (KJV)

The menu's panels have their own pages: [Verse panels]({{ '/guide/verse-panels/' | url }}) for Compare Translations and Cross-References, and [The study assistant]({{ '/guide/assistant/' | url }}) for Ask About This Passage.

## Colors

Settings › Markup holds your highlight colors. A new color, whether from the `+` in the swatch row or from Settings, is kept light enough in Light Mode, and dark enough in Dark Mode, for the text over it to stay readable. A color outside that range moves to the nearest edge of it at the same hue: a navy becomes a pale blue in Light Mode and a deep blue in Dark Mode.

A highlight made in one translation shows as a dashed underline on the whole verse when you read another.

## Bookmarks

A bookmark marks a whole verse with a glyph in the left margin. Choose Bookmark Verse in the markup menu, or press <kbd>B</kbd> with the verse selected.

## Undo

Delete a highlight, note or bookmark and <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>Z</kbd> brings it back. This works wherever you deleted it: the notes column, the markup menu or the Notes window.

## Where they show

A chapter with a highlight, note or bookmark has a ring around its number on Home, in the colors of its highlights; see [The Library]({{ '/guide/library/' | url }}). With sync on, highlights, notes and bookmarks reach your other devices.

## On the iPhone

Tap a verse to select it and open the markup menu, or press and hold to select words. For a run of marks, the highlighter turns the tab bar into a row of your colors. Both are described in [The iPhone app]({{ '/guide/iphone/' | url }}).
